Cards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Overview

Cards is a personal card builder for creating, saving, syncing, and optionally publishing game build cards. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and the choices you have.

Cards is not designed to collect sensitive personal information. Do not put private, confidential, or sensitive information into build names, notes, card text, usernames, or shared builds.

Information Cards Uses

  • Email address: used by Supabase Auth to send magic sign-in links and manage your authenticated session. Email addresses are not shown in shared build listings.
  • Username: created on first sign-in and shown next to builds you choose to publish.
  • Build content: saved cards include build names, hero names, tags, notes, selected assets, colors, equipment choices, and other card fields.
  • Published builds: when you publish a build, Cards stores a public snapshot of that build for signed-in users to view and copy. Editing your private build does not update the published snapshot until you publish changes.
  • Local browser data: Cards uses localStorage for saved build state, build library data, and magic-link cooldown timing. Supabase may also store authentication session data in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Technical logs: hosting, authentication, database, and email providers may process operational data such as IP address, browser details, request timing, delivery status, and error logs.

How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To save, load, sync, duplicate, delete, export, and publish builds.
  • To show usernames on shared builds without showing email addresses.
  • To diagnose bugs, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Cards does not sell personal information and does not use build data for advertising.

Sharing And Service Providers

Private builds are intended to be visible only to the signed-in owner. Published builds are visible to signed-in Cards users and include the publisher's username.

Cards uses service providers to run the app: Cloudflare for hosting, Supabase for authentication and database storage, and Brevo or the configured email provider for magic-link email delivery. Those providers process data only as needed to provide their services.

Your Choices

  • You can keep builds private by not publishing them.
  • You can unpublish a build to remove its shared snapshot.
  • You can delete saved builds in the app.
  • You can sign out and clear your browser storage to remove local app data from that browser.
  • You can request account, username, or cloud build deletion from the maintainer.

Retention

Cloud-synced builds and usernames are kept while your account remains active or until deletion is requested. Deleted builds may leave tombstone records so cloud sync does not accidentally restore them. Published snapshots are kept until you unpublish them or deletion is requested.

Security

Cards uses HTTPS, Supabase Auth, and Supabase row-level security policies to separate private user data. No service-role keys or private credentials are intentionally included in the browser app. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid storing sensitive content in builds.

Children

Cards is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information through Cards, contact the maintainer so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Cards changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the maintainer through the Cards GitHub repository or the channel where you received access to the app.
